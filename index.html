Settings

A tick is the unit of the door timers. With 1 tick per click and 0 per second, n and m are in clicks and waiting costs nothing. With 1 and 1, n and m are in seconds and every click fast-forwards the clock by a second.

Points distribution for each door (a JSON list; reused in order if there are more doors than entries)

Types: {"type":"quality","min":5,"max":10,"sd":5} (each door gets its own true average, drawn at random between min and max for each player; each click pays a normal draw around it)   {"type":"normal","mean":6,"sd":3}   {"type":"uniform","min":1,"max":10}   {"type":"discrete","values":[0,5,20],"weights":[5,4,1]}   {"type":"fixed","value":5}. Add "min" or "max" to cap a distribution; without a "min", points can come out negative. Points are rounded to whole numbers unless you add "round":false.
